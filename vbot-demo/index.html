Sample events are labeled synthetic. They never contact the robot. Actual game events require a fresh engine result and a matching move in the existing Lichess bot stream.

One action at a time. Rest after completion: 10 seconds for gestures, 20 seconds for dances. Full dances at least three minutes apart. Fresh readiness checks before each action. Busy moves are skipped, never queued. An uncertain robot result disarms the demo and is never retried automatically.

Disarm prevents future requests. It does not stop a dance already running. Use the onsite handset stop for that.

Live arming requires a verified application version or a local record of this exact preset being successfully tested on this robot. Sound remains a separate check. Keep this desk open on the Mac running the local coach backend.

Full-dance rules: net material gain ≥2 points after the expected reply, evaluation gain ≥2 pawns over recent turns, or a sustained ≥1.5-pawn improvement (last 3 turns versus prior 5). Depth 12+ and noise checks required. These are heuristics, not calibrated statistical significance. Fast games (estimated base + 40 increments under 10 minutes), unknown time controls, or under 2 minutes remaining use head gestures only.
